Our Story

The Art of Seeing

Founded on the belief that the most powerful images are born from genuine connection and intentional craft.

Est. 2018

A Studio Built on Light

Taran AY photography began as a small loft space flooded with afternoon light — a place where founder Elena Marchetti could explore the intersection of fine art and human emotion through photography.

What started as intimate portrait sessions for friends and local creatives has grown into a full-service boutique studio, yet our ethos remains unchanged: we create slowly, we observe deeply, and we honor the stories entrusted to us.

Our studio is a sanctuary — warm linen walls, curated props, and floor-to-ceiling windows that transform with the seasons. Every detail is considered, from the music playing softly in the background to the hand-poured tea waiting when you arrive.
