Meet Ida

A Passion for Preserving Precious Moments

Hi, I’m Ida — a maternity and newborn photographer with over 7 years of experience dedicated to preserving your family’s most tender chapters.

Photography is more than my profession; it is an art of patience, gentleness, and presence. Every mother deserves to feel radiant and at ease, and every newborn deserves a calm, unhurried space where their tiniest details are cherished.

Through specialized training and an individualized approach, my focus is always on capturing authentic emotion and creating heirloom portraits you will hold onto for generations.
