Getting Ready

A few simple things to know before your newborn session, so the day goes as smoothly and comfortably as possible for you and your little one.

Newborn Session Preparation

We're looking forward to your newborn session! To help everything go smoothly and comfortably, please follow these simple tips:

  • Feed your baby beforehand. Please feed your baby about 20–30 minutes before the session.
  • Try to keep baby a little sleepy, not overtired. If possible, try to keep your baby awake for a little while before you arrive.
  • Dress baby simply. Loose, comfortable clothing works best. Please avoid tight socks or outfits that need to be pulled over the head.
  • Bring a bottle. Please make sure to bring a bottle of milk for the baby. If you are breastfeeding, please pump milk in advance and bring it in a bottle. If your baby is formula-fed, please bring enough formula. We recommend bringing extra milk/formula in case the baby needs more during the session.
  • Bring their pacifier, if used. If your baby uses a pacifier, please bring it with you.

If you have any questions before your session, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're excited to photograph your little one!
